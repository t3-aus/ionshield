02Corrections to the Engineering Validation Report

Four arithmetic errors in the EVR change BOM items. These are corrections, not interpretations. Each is verified below. The corrected values replace the EVR and BOM-change recommendations in all subsequent sections.

Important: Corrections 1–4 below supersede the EVR recommendations for VFD-101, P-202, PSV-101, and the nozzle minimum count. The BOM_CHANGES_IMPACT_RANKED document inherits errors from the EVR on items 2, 3 and 5.

Correction 1 — P-101 hydraulic power (affects VFD-101 sizing)

EVR states: (0.203 L/min × 3.0 bar) / 600 = 0.0001515 kW → then uses 0.15 kW Actual result: 0.203 × 3.0 / 600 = 0.001015 kW (≈ 1 W) Error: EVR wrote 0.0001515 but carried forward 0.15 kW — a ×100 slip. Consequence: Motor = 0.001015 / 0.60 = 0.0017 kW theoretical. Practical minimum motor: 0.18–0.25 kW (pump mechanics and torque, not hydraulics). VFD-101: 0.37 kW (smallest standard frame, replaces EVR/BOM recommendation of 1.5 kW).

Correction 2 — P-202 compressor FAD (affects compressor sizing)

EVR formula: FAD = 232 NL/min × (3.5 + 1) / 1 = 1,044 NL/min "≈ 17.4 L/min" Error 1: NL (normal litres) are already referenced to atmospheric conditions. No pressure multiplier applies. The 1,044 NL/min result is fabricated. Error 2: 1,044 NL/min ÷ 60 = 17.4 NL/s — the "L/min" unit label is also wrong. Actual demand: 232 NL/min — this IS the FAD figure, with no multiplier. Specified: ≥300 NL/min FAD at 7–8 bar storage, 2.2–3 kW class oil-free compressor. Motor estimate: 232 × 0.012 kW/NL/min ≈ 2.8 kW (replaces EVR's 1.5 kW). 100–200 L receiver to buffer zone switching transients.

Correction 3 — PSV-101 setpoint (affects pressure protection hierarchy)

EVR/BOM-change recommendation: 3.3 bar cracking Error: P-101 must discharge above nozzle inlet + mixing losses. Nozzle pressure = 3.0 bar. With quill, static mixer and hose: P-101 discharge ≈ 3.5–4.0 barg. A PSV set at 3.3 bar lifts during normal operation. The system cannot run. Also: "Pilot-operated, 20 L/h rated" does not exist as a standard product for this service. Correct pressure ladder: Normal discharge P-101: 3.5 – 4.0 barg PAH alarm: 4.5 barg PSHH hard trip: 5.5 barg (stops P-101, closes XV-101) PSV-101 cracking: 5.8 barg (spring-loaded, PTFE-lined, return to TK-101) Chemical boundary: 6.0 barg (H02 — pressure engineer to confirm) Equipment design pressure: 8.0 barg (per BOM H02 note)

Correction 4 — Nozzle count minimum

EVR states: "8 nozzles minimum for 50 mg/L headspace target" Only three scenarios tested (2, 4, 8 nozzles). Scenario 5 and 6 were skipped. Actual minimum: ceil(12.2 L/h ÷ 2.4 L/h per nozzle) = ceil(5.08) = 6 nozzles 6 nozzles: total liquid = 14.4 L/h; carrier = 14.4 – 12.2 = 2.2 L/h ✓ POSITIVE 8 nozzles is retained as a DESIGN CHOICE for two reasons: (a) 7.0 L/h carrier headroom for concentration trim across 25–50 mg/L range (b) 2 × 4-nozzle zones allow single-zone partial treatment and fault isolation

04Mass Balance — Verified Backward Calculation

The EVR mass balance is arithmetically correct. These numbers are reproduced here as the governing figures for all equipment sizing.

Step 1 — Total PAA mass: 50 mg/L × 25,000 L ÷ 1,000,000 = 1.25 kg PAA required per treatment Step 2 — Injection rate: 1.25 kg ÷ 6 h = 0.208 kg/h Step 3 — Stock flow (P-101): PAA in stock = 1.5% × 1.14 kg/L ÷ 100 = 0.0171 kg/L Stock flow = 0.208 ÷ 0.0171 = 12.2 L/h ✓ within P-101 range (5–15 L/h) Step 4 — Nozzle flow balance (8 nozzles): Total liquid = 8 × 2.4 = 19.2 L/h Carrier water = 19.2 − 12.2 = 7.0 L/h ✓ positive Total air = 8 × 29 = 232 NL/min ✓ Step 5 — Mixed concentration: Ct = (0.015 × 12.2) ÷ (12.2 + 7.0) = 0.183 ÷ 19.2 = 0.953% w/w ≈ 9,530 mg/L liquid Sensitivity — 25 mg/L scenario: Stock flow = 6.1 L/h → 6 nozzles sufficient → air = 174 NL/min Sensitivity — 75 mg/L scenario: Stock flow = 18.3 L/h → EXCEEDS P-101 max (15 L/h) → Extend contact time to 9 h, OR use higher-strength stock 75 mg/L is OUT OF SCOPE for current P-101 spec without modification.
EVR "discrepancy" note: The EVR flags 7.3 mg/L headspace as a discrepancy versus the 50 mg/L target. This is not an error — 7.3 mg/L is the per-hour dose. Integrated over 6 h with no losses, it gives 43.8 mg/L, approaching 50 mg/L when liquid density is included. The "discrepancy" arises from omitting the time dimension. The dose controller in §6 tracks cumulative mass, which is the correct closure.

06Control Architecture — Five Loops

The outer dose controller is the critical addition missing from the BOM. It converts a flow-controlled system into a treatment-validated system, which is what closes the loop on H08.

FIC-101 — Stock Flow (primary)

FT-101 Coriolis → PLC compares to setpoint → VFD-101 adjusts P-101 speed and stroke. Setpoint comes from the dose controller. Accuracy target: ±2% at 12.2 L/h steady state. Soft-start 2–3 s ramp on start.

FIC-201 — Carrier Flow (ratio)

Carrier setpoint = total nozzle capacity target minus FT-101 live reading. Tecpro VFD holds 4.5 bar header; FCV-201 trims flow to match ratio. FT-201 provides feedback. FCV-201 has no independent setpoint authority (H03 closure).

PIC-202 — Air Pressure

PCV-202 holds 3.0 barg at nozzle header via internal spring. PLC monitors PT-202; alarms on deviation >0.2 bar. Zone air valves open/close without affecting header pressure — this is why the receiver is mandatory.

AIC-201 — Concentration Trim

AIT-201 sample-loop reads mixed liquid concentration. PLC compares to target (0.953% w/w). Slow trim loop (integral-only, τ_i ≈ 5 min) adjusts FCV-201 carrier setpoint. Not the primary control variable — the dose controller is.

DIC-001 — Dose Controller (outer / master)

Integrates FT-101 reading × stock concentration (0.0171 kg/L, updated by H01 assay) over time → cumulative PAA mass delivered. Run ends when mass target is reached (default 1.25 kg for 50 mg/L). This loop does not rely on a headspace sensor and is closed by chemistry and flow measurement alone. It is the direct answer to the BOM-change AIT-201 ambiguity: liquid measurement is used to calculate and verify dose; headspace validation (H08) is a separate field step requiring a gas-phase sensor not on this skid.

07P&ID Narrative

This is a line-by-line narrative pending a formal drawn P&ID. Tags are consistent with the EVR and BOM. Lines shown as dashed are control signals (4–20 mA or PROFINET); solid lines are process.

Skid 101 — Chemical Dosing

TK-101 → XV-101: Gravity or pump suction draws 1.5% PAA through a 100 µm suction strainer. XV-101 (NC solenoid, 24 VDC) is the first isolator — it closes on E-stop, PSHH, or comms loss. A manual isolation valve and sample point sit immediately upstream of XV-101.

XV-101 → P-101: PTFE-diaphragm metering pump, PVDF head, 0–20 L/h at 8 barg. Motor 0.18–0.25 kW driven by VFD-101 (0.37 kW). Speed and stroke both modulated by FIC-101. A pulsation damper is recommended on the discharge side before FT-101.

P-101 → FT-101 → PT-101/PSV-101: Micro-Coriolis FT-101 is the primary flow feedback. PT-101 monitors discharge pressure; its signal feeds both FIC-101 (for pump cavitation detection) and the PSHH-101 trip at 5.5 barg. PSV-101 (5.8 barg, spring-loaded, PTFE-lined) returns to TK-101 via a contained bypass — sized at 1.25× P-101 max flow (≈19 L/h).

FT-101 outlet → H-1 transfer hose: PTFE 6.35 mm ID, rated ≥10 barg, 316SS fittings with Viton ferrules, 10–25 m long. Pressure drop at 12.2 L/h: <0.02 bar (negligible). Both ends have manual isolation and a check valve to prevent backflow.

Skid 201 — Atomisation

Mains → WBT-201 → Tecpro Multistage: Float-controlled fill to WBT-201 (200 L PE, air-gap fill). The Tecpro multistage draws from WBT-201 and maintains 4.5 barg on the carrier header via its integral VFD and pressure transducer. A minimum-flow recirculation line (FO-recirc, orifice-sized) returns to WBT-201 to keep the pump above its minimum stable flow at all times, including during the post-treatment flush cycle.

Tecpro discharge → FCV-201 → FT-201: FCV-201 (micro-proportional, Cv ≈ 0.01) trims carrier flow. FT-201 (micro-Coriolis) provides the carrier flow feedback signal to FIC-201. A 25 µm strainer sits immediately upstream of FCV-201.

H-1 arrival → quill → check valve → MX-201: Stock arrives at 3.5–4.0 barg and injects into the carrier stream via a 316L quill. A PTFE check valve on the quill prevents carrier backflow into the stock line. MX-201 (8-element 316L static mixer, L/D ≥ 40) ensures complete homogenisation before the AIT-201 sample point.

MX-201 → AIT-201 sample → Zone valves → Nozzles: A slipstream sample is drawn to AIT-201 via a small PTFE loop with a peristaltic sample pump and return. The main stream splits into Zone A and Zone B at two 316L tee manifolds, each with a 24 VDC NC solenoid zone valve (XV-ZA, XV-ZB). Each zone feeds 4 × AKIMist 03C TN nozzles via a 316L distribution manifold with individual PTFE check valves and pressure gauges.

Air train: P-202 (oil-free, 7–8 bar) → 100–200 L receiver → 0.01 µm coalescing filter and refrigerant dryer → PCV-202 (3.0 barg set) → air PSV (5.0 barg, discharges to atmosphere) → zone air solenoids (XV-AA, XV-AB, 24 VDC) → nozzle air inlets. Zone liquid and zone air valves are sequenced: air opens 2 s before liquid on start; air stays open 60 s after liquid closes on stop (blowdown).

10Operating Sequence

Start-up

Pre-start checks

Verify TK-101 level (LAH between 20% and 90%), WBT-201 level, bund dry, all manual isolations open, E-stop reset. Enter batch parameters: PPM target, contact time, pile void volume.

Air on first

P-202 starts → receiver charges to 7–8 bar → PCV-202 holds 3.0 barg on nozzle header. Confirm PT-202 ≥ 2.8 bar before proceeding. Zone air valves XV-AA and XV-AB open.

Carrier water on

Tecpro multistage starts → FCV-201 opens to initial carrier setpoint. FT-201 confirms flow within ±10% of setpoint before P-101 is permitted to start.

Liquid zone valves open

XV-ZA and XV-ZB open. Nozzles now receive carrier water only — confirms nozzle hydraulics before chemical is introduced.

P-101 ramp

XV-101 opens. P-101 ramps from 0 to stock setpoint (12.2 L/h) over 2–3 s. FIC-101 enters closed-loop control. Dose controller begins accumulating mass (FT-101 × 0.0171 kg/L × dt).

Normal run

Dose controller runs to mass target (1.25 kg for 50 mg/L, 6 h). FIC-201 adjusts carrier to maintain constant total flow to nozzles. AIC-201 trims carrier setpoint slowly if liquid concentration drifts. PLC logs FT-101, FT-201, PT-101, PT-202, AIT-201 at 10 s intervals to batch record.

Normal stop

P-101 off, XV-101 close

Dose target reached. P-101 stops. XV-101 closes. Stock line is now isolated.

Carrier flush

Carrier continues at nozzle-limited flow for 2–5 min, purging residual stock from hose H-1, quill, MX-201 and nozzle manifold. PLC monitors FT-201; flush continues until AIT-201 reads <50 mg/L or timer expires.

Air blowdown

Carrier stops. Zone air solenoids stay open for 60 s at 3.0 bar, clearing all liquid from nozzle orifices and manifold branches. Prevents crystallisation.

System safe state

All zone valves closed. Tecpro multistage stops. P-202 coasts to stop. Batch record saved with total mass delivered, duration, and peak pressures.

Trip response (any hardwired ESD)

XV-101 closes + P-101 STO in <50 ms. Zone liquid valves close. Air blowdown initiates if compressor still running (60 s). PLC logs trip cause and timestamp. System latches — manual reset required after fault cleared.
