Ion Shield Pilot: Skid 101 / Skid 201

Conceptual P&ID and general arrangement, Rev A. Not for fabrication. Basis: BOM01 and Engineering Validation Report: 12.2 L/h stock + 7.0 L/h carrier = 19.2 L/h to 8 × AKIMist 03C TN; air 232 NL/min at 3.0 bar; 50 mg/L target.

1. Preliminary P&ID

  • Loops: FIC-101 holds stock flow. FIC-201 sets carrier = 19.2 − FT-101. PIC-202 holds air at 3.0 bar. The outer dose controller integrates delivered PAA mass and ends the run at target. AIT-201 verifies and trims only.
  • Pressure ladder (chemical side): normal ≈ 3.6-4.0 barg, PAH 4.5, PSHH-101 trip 5.0, PSV-101 5.5, boundary 6 barg. Air header PSV-202 5.0 barg.
  • Shutdown: P-101 off, carrier flushes 2-5 min at nozzle-limited flow (≈0.3 L/min), then 60 s air blowdown. Trips: PSHH, air < 2.5 bar, dry-run, bund leak, comms loss, E-stop.

2. General arrangement (plan view, to scale)

  • Envelopes are assumptions: Skid 101 ≈ 1.5 × 1.0 m, Skid 201 ≈ 2.2 × 1.0 m. Tank and compressor sit low; PAA pipework stays above the bund and clear of the panel.
  • Panels face the service side with 600 mm clearance. Utilities enter at one boundary point per skid. Forklift pockets and corner tie-downs are on both skids.
